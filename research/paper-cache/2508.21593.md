# [2508.21593] Growing Mathlib: maintenance of a large scale mathematical library

- arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.21593

## Abstract

Abstract:Mathlib is the community-maintained mathematical library for Lean 4, comprising roughly a million lines of formalized mathematics contributed by hundreds of collaborators. This paper describes the engineering and organizational practices behind maintaining mathlib at scale: continuous integration, automated refactoring, dependency management, the role of Lean's typeclass system in inferring implicit arguments, and tooling for large-scale collaboration. We discuss the challenges of a rapidly growing library (breaking changes, porting from Lean 3, performance), and the practices that have allowed mathlib to become the largest and most active formal mathematics library.
