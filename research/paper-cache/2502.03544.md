# [2502.03544] Gold-medalist Performance in Solving Olympiad Geometry with AlphaGeometry2

- arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.03544

## Abstract

Abstract:We present AlphaGeometry2, a significantly improved version of AlphaGeometry, which advances the state of the art in solving Olympiad geometry problems. The system combines a neuro-symbolic architecture: a language model proposes auxiliary constructions, and a symbolic engine performs deductive search, trained without human demonstrations. AlphaGeometry2 solves 84% of all geometry problems from the past 25 years of the International Mathematical Olympiad (IMO), up from 54% for AlphaGeometry, and achieved gold-medalist-level performance. At IMO 2024, it solved the geometry problem (P4), contributing to the combined DeepMind system's silver-medal score alongside AlphaProof.
